Every few months someone says agents are on the way out. The newest data says the opposite. According to the National Association of Realtors 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, 90% of buyers and 92% of sellers used an agent this year. Both numbers are record highs.

So buyers still want an agent. What has changed is how they pick one. At Real Effect Media, we work with agents nationwide, and we see the same pattern in every market: the agent who shows up first online usually gets the call.

## What the 2026 NAR report found

Demand for agents is at an all time high. Here are the numbers that matter most for your marketing:

- **90% of buyers used an agent.** That's a record. Back in 2001 it was 69%.
- **92% of sellers used an agent,** up from 91% last year.
- **FSBO sales are at 5%,** tied with the all time low. At the 1985 peak, it was 21%.
- **76% of buyers rated their agent very useful,** and 63% leaned on their agent as a frequent source of information.
- **Agent assisted homes sold for a median of $455,000,** compared to $368,500 for FSBO sales.

The takeaway is simple. Buyers and sellers aren't trying to skip the agent. They're trying to find the right one.

## How buyers choose an agent now

Before a buyer ever picks up the phone, they search. They look up neighborhoods, school districts, what homes cost and what it's like to live there. Most of those searches land on Google and YouTube.

Whoever answers those questions earns trust before the first conversation. By the time the buyer reaches out, they already feel like they know that agent.

That's the gap Real Effect Media sees in almost every market we research. Plenty of agents are great at their job, but when you search their city, they don't show up. Someone else does, and that someone gets the call.

If 90% of buyers are going to hire an agent anyway, the real competition is visibility.

## Why YouTube is the answer

YouTube is the second largest search engine in the world, and Google owns it. A good YouTube video can rank on YouTube and in Google results at the same time. One piece of content, two places to be found.

It also does something a portal ad can't. When you pay for leads on a portal, the attention disappears the moment you stop paying. A YouTube channel is an asset you own. A video you post this month can still bring in buyers two years from now.

There's one more reason video matters right now. Social feeds are filling up with generic AI content, and buyers can tell. Real video of a real agent who knows the market stands out more than ever. People hire people they trust, and nothing builds trust faster than seeing and hearing you before you meet.
