## What YouTube Hype Is and Why It Matters

Hype is a button that lets viewers do more than like a video. When someone hypes a video, it earns points, and the videos with the most points climb a public Hype leaderboard that YouTube ranks by region. Getting on that leaderboard means exposure to viewers who have never heard of you.

Here's the part that matters for agents: Hype is only for smaller channels. It applies to long form videos from channels in the YouTube Partner Program with between 500 and 500,000 subscribers. Big creators are shut out entirely.

Even better, YouTube weights the points in favor of the little guy. The fewer subscribers you have, the more each hype is worth. A channel with 2,000 subscribers can get more lift from a handful of hypes than a channel with 300,000.

YouTube tested Hype in 2024 and rolled it out globally in 2025. The message from YouTube is clear: they want to help emerging channels reach new audiences, and they are giving fans a direct way to make that happen.

For a real estate agent, think about who those fans are. Past clients. Your sphere. Local business owners you work with. People who already like you and want to see you win. Every one of them can push your videos up the leaderboard in your region.

## Real Estate Is One of the Highest Paying Niches on YouTube

Advertisers pay very different rates depending on who is watching. Real estate sits near the top of the list. vidIQ estimates real estate and mortgage content pulls CPMs in the range of $15 to $40, which puts it in the highest paying tier on the platform.

The reason is simple. Mortgage lenders, home search platforms, and real estate software companies spend heavily to reach people who are about to make a six figure decision. Your viewers are exactly the audience they want.

But here's the thing most people miss. For an agent, ad revenue is a bonus, not the goal. One viewer who calls you to list their home or help them relocate is worth more than any ad check YouTube will ever send. A video with 1,500 views from people actually moving to your city is worth more than 100,000 views from people who will never buy a house there.

## Most Agents Are Still Ignoring YouTube

According to the National Association of REALTORS 2024 Technology Survey, 87 percent of agents use Facebook in their business and 62 percent use Instagram. Only 25 percent use YouTube.

And using YouTube is not the same as using it well. Plenty of agents uploaded a listing tour once, got 40 views, and never posted again.

Meanwhile, buyers are on YouTube researching neighborhoods, cost of living, schools, and what it's really like to live somewhere. They are looking for answers, and in most markets nobody local is giving them.

That means in most markets across the country, the top spot for searches like "moving to" your city or "best neighborhoods in" your city is wide open. The agent who claims it first gets a head start that is very hard to catch.

## How Agents Can Get More Business From YouTube

**Show up where buyers and sellers are searching.** YouTube is the second largest search engine in the world, and Google regularly shows YouTube videos in its search results. A well titled video about your market can bring in leads for years.

**Build trust before the first call.** When someone has watched you walk through three neighborhoods and explain the local market, they feel like they already know you. By the time they reach out, you're not competing with five other agents. They've already picked you.

**Own your leads instead of renting them.** Portal leads stop the moment you stop paying. A YouTube channel is an asset you own, and it keeps working and compounding long after each video goes live.

**Use Shorts for reach and long videos for trust.** Short videos get you discovered. Long videos turn viewers into clients. And long videos are the ones that qualify for Hype.

**Let your fans do the promoting.** Ask past clients and your sphere to hype your videos. Because you're a smaller channel, each hype carries extra weight, and that can land you on the regional leaderboard in front of brand new viewers.

**Put your videos to work on Google too.** Posting your videos to your Google Business Profile helps you stand out in local search and on Maps, right where people are looking for an agent.
