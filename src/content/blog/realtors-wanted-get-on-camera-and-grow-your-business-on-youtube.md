## How to Record Great Video From Home

You don't need expensive gear. A recent smartphone and a few simple tweaks will get you footage that looks professional once Real Effect Media edits it.

**Camera**

- Use your phone's **back camera**, not the selfie camera. It's much sharper.
- Film **horizontally** for long videos and vertically for Shorts.
- Set your phone to **4K or 1080p** at 30 frames per second.
- Put it on a tripod at **eye level**, about an arm's length or two away. A basic phone tripod is plenty.

**Lighting**

- **Face a window.** Soft daylight in front of you is the easiest flattering light there is.
- Never sit with a bright window **behind** you, or you'll turn into a silhouette.
- Turn off overhead lights if they cast harsh shadows. A simple ring light or softbox helps on cloudy days.

**Sound**

Audio matters more than video. Viewers will forgive a slightly soft image, but not bad sound.

- A **clip on lavalier mic** is the single best upgrade you can make, and good ones are affordable.
- Record in a room with carpet, curtains, or furniture. Empty rooms echo.
- Turn off fans, AC, and notifications before you hit record.

**Background**

- If we're placing you on a custom background, stand in front of a **plain wall** with some space between you and it. Ask us if you want a green screen recommendation.
- Otherwise, keep it clean and simple: a tidy shelf or a plant looks great. Avoid clutter.

**On camera tips**

- Look at the **lens**, not the screen.
- Smile before you start talking and pause for a beat after you finish. It gives us clean edit points.
- Mess up a line? Don't stop. Pause, then say it again. Real Effect Media will cut the best take.
- Wear solid colors. Skip tiny patterns and stripes, which can flicker on camera.
- **Energy up.** Camera flattens your personality a bit, so bring about 20% more than feels natural.

## How to Start Your YouTube Channel the Right Way

If you don't have a channel yet, setting one up takes about 15 minutes. Here's how to do it so it's built for growth from day one.

1. **Create a Brand Account.** Set up your channel as a Brand Account under your Google account, not a personal channel. You stay the Owner, and you can add Real Effect Media as a Manager so we can upload and optimize without ever needing your password.
2. **Name it for your market.** Use your name, your market, or both, like "Jane Smith | Denver Real Estate." Make it easy for local viewers to know who you are and where you work.
3. **Write a keyword rich About section.** Mention your city, the neighborhoods you serve, and what viewers will learn from your channel.
4. **Add your contact info and links.** Include your website, phone, and Google Business Profile so viewers can reach you in one click.
5. **Brand it.** Upload a clear headshot as your profile photo and a banner with your name, market, and a short tagline. Real Effect Media can design these for you.
6. **Plan a consistent schedule.** One quality video a week beats ten videos one month and nothing the next. YouTube rewards channels that viewers keep coming back to.

**Video ideas that work for agents**

- Pros and cons of living in a specific neighborhood or suburb
- Cost of living breakdowns for your city
- Moving to your area: what newcomers need to know
- Monthly market updates explained in plain English
- First time buyer mistakes and how to avoid them
- How to price and prep a home to sell
- Short form Shorts with quick tips, local spots, and new listings
